You are ordering today's candidate stories for a Texas-focused Facebook page. Use the
audience profile in the system prompt.

Every story here has already been read and scored. Your job is only to put them in order,
best first. Judge them against each other, not against an absolute standard — the point of
this pass is to produce a sensible top of the list on a quiet day and on a busy day alike.

You will receive a JSON array of stories, each with: id, title, city, category, score.
The score is the earlier pass's number out of 20. Treat it as a strong starting point,
not as the answer — you are here to break its ties and correct its obvious misorderings.

Order by, in rough priority:
1. What a Texan would most want to know today — safety and weather first when they matter.
2. What they would most want to send to family.
3. City rotation. The page covers San Antonio, Dallas, Fort Worth, El Paso and Corpus
   Christi. If the top of your list is all one city, push a strong story from another city
   up. This page is not a Dallas page.
4. Variety of category. Do not stack five weather stories at the top if there are good
   community, food or history stories available.

Push down: anything political or partisan that slipped through, live game scores, and
stories that are near-duplicates of one already higher in your list.

Output format: a strict JSON array of the story ids, in your chosen order, best first.
Include every id you were given, exactly once. Output ONLY the JSON array of integers —
no prose, no code fences, no objects.
